# Muscle Band, Light Ball, and Quick Claw

The supplied O06 item sheet shows Muscle Band in thunderstorm, Light Ball in intense sun, and Quick Claw in heavy rain. The earlier Master Edition video review also observed a thunderstorm Quick Claw. The O06 original is `research-assets/user-older-edition-2026-11-04/share_33ea1aae55bf09c2a708ecde60b1e38b.png`, SHA-256 `38da1271a818c3dca1556f0046b9e3675ba44b0f54eac4ec6f34c0a227ebd845`.

Muscle Band uses two distinct hand cards as a virtual command for one of the holder’s moves. It follows the ordinary move allowance, Power, and defense rules. The virtual command has no printed weather, so Paralysis prevents using it, as with Justified. The booklet distinguishes using a command from playing an actual command as an event response.

Light Ball discards itself during Play and then attempts to apply Paralysis. Defiant can resolve between the discard and the status. Shield Dust, Limber, Poison Touch, and Articuno continue to apply. The preview interprets the unqualified Pokémon target as a living active Pokémon. The booklet reserves some reserve interactions for effects that permit them explicitly.

Quick Claw removes distance restrictions from the trainer’s card use and the holder’s moves. At another trainer’s turn start, it offers one actual clear or intense-sun command against that trainer’s active Pokémon. The preview offers these windows in seat order after the existing start-of-turn status and trait effects and before Prep and Draw. Later holders skip the window if the original target has been replaced. The turn owner’s command allowance belongs to their own Play phase. The command and move choices are visible only to the holder, who can pass with any hand.

All eleven held items in the saved O06 sheet now resolve in combat preview. Isolated fixtures supply actual item cards for testing. The retail deck still needs verified copy counts by card and weather.

Browser QA used three trainers and a spectator. A trainer paid two cards to use Scratch with Muscle Band, spent Light Ball and passed Defiant before the target became Paralyzed, and used Quick Claw before another trainer drew. That trainer completed both normal draws after the interrupt. Only the Quick Claw holder saw the command choices.
