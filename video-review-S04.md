## Open questions and implementation implications

- Confirm against the official rulebook: whether and how effects can place statuses on a Pokémon in reserve; what happens when that Pokémon is switched in; and which tokens/statuses are discarded on switching.
- Resolve the level-up optional/mandatory conflict, rescue sequence and simultaneous-win priority, player-count setup, move limits, and card-specific exceptions from authoritative rules/card text.
- Treat creator narration and 三国杀 analogies as secondary evidence. The video does not establish a definitive retail edition, complete card catalogue, card quantities, or all edge cases. Keep uncertain rules configurable/unimplemented until corroborated.
