The command's trainer must then discard one actual own hand card or that command's move has no effect on this Pokémon. Passing the initial optional payment grants no protection. The source payment is a discard, not another command use; either real spare card may satisfy it. When self-targeting, the two payments require two distinct cards in addition to the command card already used. No hand card means the target cannot activate; no spare source card means the protected target is automatically unaffected after the target pays. Native weather-paid moves do not trigger Pressure. Virtual Take this! use does.

Explicit target-assignment policy: finish Cute Charm redirects and the existing Moxie gain, then process final command targets in their current list order before Future Sight, any move-use target effect, and defense. Each original target instance is offered Pressure once for the whole command, not once per hit. Bind an unpaid protection to that same original target for every settlement of the command, including repeated hits. Other targets continue normally. Remove a protected original target from the effective target list before move-use effects, so neither target-marker removal nor Freeze-Dry suppression is applied to it. Command payment, use allowance, Power consumption and already captured damage boosts remain spent; there is no refund or new command. Gluttony reads: 若此宝可梦已携带持有物，此持有物效果改为“在你的回合结束时，携带此持有物的宝可梦回复1点体力”. It uses 回复, not 恢复, and replaces the held item's effect. It does not replace its name, physical identity, card kind or zone, transfer ownership, discard the held card, or create a new item. Booklet07 p09 states that a held-item card stays in the Pokémon's area, one item per Pokémon, and replacement discards the previous item. This trait cannot convert an arbitrary play card or tool into a held item. No unsupported item quantity or effect is inferred. Only an actual effective held-item card on a living active Snorlax supplies the replacement effect; an ineffective held item supplies no heal.

While effective Gluttony replaces Rocky Helmet's effect, Helmet does not retaliate.
