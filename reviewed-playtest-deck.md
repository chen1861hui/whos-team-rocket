# Reviewed cards playtest deck

In a lobby, the host can select **Reviewed cards** under **Playtest deck** and save the room settings. The choice is visible to the room and appears in the start confirmation. It carries into the match through identity assignment and Pokémon selection. New rooms and legacy saves use the basic deck by default.

The reviewed deck adds eight command cards and six Dodges per trainer to a shared pile. Each trainer contributes three intense-sun, two clear, two heavy-rain, and one thunderstorm command. The Dodge mix is two intense-sun, two clear, one heavy-rain, and one thunderstorm per trainer. These are deck quantities, so each trainer still receives a shuffled four-card opening hand.

The shared pile also contains two copies of each of the five split tool pairs, one of each of the ten events, and one of each of the eleven held items. The total ranges from 73 cards at three trainers to 115 at six. The deck includes Articuno’s Protection and enables its reaction windows. Tool, event, and item weather comes from the saved face reviews. The basic deck uses six commands and six Dodges per trainer, all intense sun.

The optional deck is a fixture with quantities chosen for playtesting. It leaves the retail manifest gates in place. The room stores the choice for recovery and rematches, and the active match stores which deck it actually started with. Opponents and spectators receive the deck profile and public counts without receiving the deck order or private hands.

Browser QA used a fresh room with three trainers and a spectator. The host saved the deck from the lobby, and the spectator saw the saved choice. All trainers acknowledged their identities, drew 15 distinct Pokémon candidates across the room, and selected partners. Four game cards went to each trainer, leaving 61 cards in the shared draw pile. The spectator received none of the private hands. The first trainer drew twice and equipped a held item from the shuffled deal.
