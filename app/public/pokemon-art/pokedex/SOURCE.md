# Pokédex artwork

The Pokédex uses the original 2D illustrations from the [PokéAPI artwork archive](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/tree/c0542f7c51215d9672439012838c99f520596cbd/sprites/pokemon/other/official-artwork). PNG filenames are National Pokédex numbers. The copies are resized to fit within 320 × 320 pixels.
