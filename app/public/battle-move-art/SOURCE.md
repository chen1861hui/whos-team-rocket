# Battle move textures

These grayscale masks are PNG conversions of files in the local Pokémon: Let's Go, Pikachu! / Eevee! `LGPE-Misc.7z` archive.

| File | Archive entry | Arena usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ice.png` | `battle/waza/ew058_at_beam/fxmt_3_ice001_m.dds` | Ice Beam surface and frost |
| `flow.png` | `battle/waza/ew529_bullet01/fxmt_3_flow006_m.dds` | Water and flame material flow |
| `ring.png` | `battle/waza/ew022_whip00_u/fxmt_0_circle001_m.dds` | Impact and ability highlights |
| `leaf.png` | `battle/waza/ew073_df_sprout01/fxmt_0_object202_a.dds` | Leaf and pollen silhouettes |
| `laser.png` | `battle/waza/ew058_at_beam/fxmt_3_laser001_m.dds` | Beam modulation |
| `shock.png` | `battle/waza/ew058_at_beam/fxmt_3_shock001_m.dds` | Contact streaks |

The arena assembles the effects and their timings in `battlefield-move-effects.js`.
