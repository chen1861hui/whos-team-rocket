## Implementation dependencies and bounds

- All nine move conditions are command markers, requiring use of one 接招吧！ per existing reviewed booklet rules, not discard of a weather card.
- All nine printed numerals are recorded as printedValue, separate from triggered effects, weather checks and unresolved heading symbols.
- After-damage effects require actual positive damage, ordered after fainting/rescue resolution. Damage-source identity must survive pending decisions and fainting.
- Current all-intense-sun development deck naturally makes Static's not-intense-sun branch, thunderstorm paralysis and heavy-rain poison unavailable. That is a scenario constraint, not evidence of zero card effects.
- 沙隐 sunny judgment can operate in the all-intense-sun scenario if the no-held-item predicate and virtual-Dodge response are explicitly integrated. Its activation is not a generic once-per-Play trait action.
- 不服输 cannot be triggered naturally by a command/dodge-only scenario without held items. Preserve it in source data; do not fabricate held-item loss or remove the printed trait.
- 拍落/小偷 require private hand-card selection, atomic discard/transfer, source-trainer targeting, retry-safe continuation and hidden-information handling; their familiar videogame names do not establish held-item removal.
- 劈瓦/近身战 reinforcement removal happens when used. Support removable reinforcement markers and use-time ordering before defense/damage. Root should resolve choice/no-marker semantics from the accepted rules basis before enabling ambiguous interactions.
- 近身战 requires up to two legal targets, per-target defense/settlement and one reinforcement removed from each target at use time, including a defended settlement if the reviewed timing still applies.
- No complete retail quantities/weather distribution are established by these photos.
