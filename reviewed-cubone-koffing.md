## Booklet evidence and policies

Booklet08 p11 prohibits simultaneous duplicate reinforcement markers unless specified otherwise. Defense changes damage above1 to1, and when a card finishes, discards itself if that Pokémon was damaged by the card. No exception says the marker must have been present before damage. Therefore Defense newly gained by Armor is retained between hits but discarded at that damaging card's end. Cardless turn-start damage has no card-end cleanup. A redundant paid grant is allowed as a no-op beyond the one existing marker; the face does not prohibit payment. After-dealing effects precede after-receiving, so move Poison judgments settle before Armor, and damage/held-item queues preserve their existing ordering and nested rescue.

Bonemerang says after first settlement, not after causing damage. It offers once after Dodge/zero damage too. It waits for all first-settlement source/receiving/nested effects, then allows one real hand-card payment before whole-card cleanup. It targets the original post-redirection active instance, with a separate second defense and no extra command payment. The existing conservative policy stops later hits if original source or target leaves active/faints; this source-stoppage policy is not additional printed text. Bone Rush's use-count override is move text, not a trait; each use still pays a command and keeps the booklet's one command-using Pokémon constraint.

Booklet08 p10 orders different trainers' same-timing effects current-trainer-first counterclockwise, while the same trainer chooses their own order. Current trainer's status/returned cards/Gas are grouped; other Koffings follow in stored seat order. Suppression covers the chosen trainer's active and reserve partners through switching and lasts through End even if its source leaves. Revalidate later queued actors after earlier suppression; this is an explicit event-handling policy. Gas is offered once per captured instance per turn-start event, has no weather payment and does not erase or disable moves, statuses, items or reinforcement markers. Already resolved permissions with explicit turn duration remain as completed grants; new suppressed activation is unavailable. Empty Gas is skipped at its opportunity; a same-timing returned card can fund Gas when return is chosen first.
