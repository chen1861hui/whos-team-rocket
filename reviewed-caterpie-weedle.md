# Reviewed Caterpie and Weedle effects

2026-10-29. Six faces were transcribed from saved S05 originals by Sol and visually checked by root in the preceding review batch. Remaining booklet interactions were audited independently this continuation. Edition unidentified; English is a working UI translation. Exact Chinese, HP, move conditions and SHA256 provenance are in reviewed-caterpie-weedle.json and catalogue effectProvenance. Research photos are not served as cards.

Caterpie/Metapod/Butterfree have HP3/4/4 and are Disruptive. Weedle/Kakuna/Beedrill have HP3/4/4 and are Offensive. Caterpie/Metapod Tackle costs one command and deals1, with no paragraph. Weedle/Kakuna Poison Sting costs one command and deals1; after positive damage, a HEAVY RAIN ONLY judgment causes Poison. Default target count is one active Pokémon within move range, including self.

Shield Dust on both Lv1 faces prevents all new special statuses before global Poison Touch replacement. It does not cure an already-carried status. Shed Skin on both Lv2 faces optionally discards any number of actual own hand cards while receiving damage, reducing damage by1 each. No once cap, sunny restriction, draw or damage-based payment cap is printed. Resolve source modifiers first, then allow the target trainer to order receiving modifiers. This covers moves, Poison, confusion and Helmet. Zero damage suppresses after-damage effects and does not consume Defense. Choosing zero cards is an explicit harmless policy.

Butterfree Compound Eyes stops other trainers using actual intense-sun Dodges. Clear/rainy Dodges, source-owner self Dodge and successful Sand Veil remain valid. Sand Veil's virtual Dodge has no weather; its judgment card does not grant weather to the virtual card (explicit interpretation). Whirlwind index0 discards one rainy hand card of any kind, deals no damage and ignores distance for that move. It swaps the target's active and reserve. Zone rules preserve injuries/status/items, discard outgoing reinforcement and reveal incoming reserve. A missing/dead reserve makes it a no-op; forced swapping does not consume or depend on voluntary switching (explicit interpretations). Sleep Powder index1 discards one sunny hand card, deals no damage, and applies Sleep if its final judgment is NOT thunderstorm. Existing Drought, Poison Touch, Shield Dust, one-status and original-instance rules apply.

Beedrill Sniper passively removes trainer-card and Pokémon-move distance while active. Fury Attack costs one command and deals1 per settlement. On use, reveal four actual top cards. Each sunny card (intense sun OR clear) adds one settlement, so the move resolves1–5 times. This is public reveal, not judgment; Drought cannot replace it. Booklet07 p8 expressly gives repeated settlements against the same target and separate Dodges. Each hit finishes defense, damage, rescue and after-damage effects before the next. Power lasts the whole use; Defense cleanup waits until the whole card ends.

Booklet08 p10 defines reveal disposal: cards remain public until the current settlement completes, then still-revealed cards enter discard. Holding Fury's on-use reveal until whole-move cleanup is an explicit interpretation of that boundary. Actual cards remain isolated, with safe public face stubs, and discard once. Refill discard beneath existing draw cards before a four-card operation. If fewer than four total cards exist, reveal available cards (finite exhaustion policy). Stop remaining hits if the original source or target leaves active/faints; never substitute a reserve. Target binding follows same-target wording; source stoppage is a conservative engine policy rather than an explicit booklet instruction.

An audit reproduced an empty-deck stall: four held reveals left no card for a mandatory defeat reward, so the final rescue pass could not finish. The reward now draws0 when no real card is available and continues cleanup, consistent with existing Pickup exhaustion policy. It does not recycle held reveals early. Ordinary requested base draws remain strict. Sand Veil is not offered without a possible real judgment card.
