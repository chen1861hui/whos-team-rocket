# Gameplay milestone 4 — command battles through a real result

Implemented 2026-10-27 in C:\src\team-rocket-web. The supplied Chinese rulebook is the rules basis. The accepted stadium renderer, seating, cameras and themes were retained. This is a **playable development combat preview**, not the complete original deck or roster.

## What is playable

From the lobby, ready 3–6 trainers and choose **Start combat preview**. The confirmation states the scenario's scope and test deck. The original 2–6-player foundation start remains available separately and keeps its unplayable fixtures; existing saved rooms remain recoverable.

The combat preview uses three reviewed starter groups: Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle, each with all three photographed levels. Each trainer privately picks two different groups from those three. This three-candidate scenario, repeated groups across trainers and the artificial deck composition are development fixtures, not claims about the source game's five-candidate/40-group setup or physical quantities.

The test deck has **6 command and 6 dodge cards per seat**, shuffled, with every card carrying the **intense-sun** weather shown in the supplied command/dodge faces. These counts are explicit scenario choices, not inferred retail counts. Starting hands are four, ordinary draws two, hand limit four. Judgment consumes/reveals a real top card and discards it; depleted piles recycle discards without in-flight cards. No Potion, tools, held items or event cards are generated by this deck. Consequently Rain Dish's rain branch is implemented/tested with a source-derived judgment fixture but cannot naturally trigger in the all-sun preview.

## Authoritative rules included

- Random private roles for 3–6 players use booklet p.2 counts. Leader reveals and begins in seat 1; other seats retain their cyclic order. Leader active starts Lv.2; others/reserves Lv.1. At 5–6 trainers both leader partners have +1 max HP. HP values are accepted only from the nine photo-reviewed faces.
- Command commitment validates own Play, a real owned command card, active creature, move use limit and legal distinct targets. Default targets include self and adjacent living trainers' active Pokémon; Surf at Lv.3 may target up to two. Elimination removes seats from range. Command-user identity remains locked across switching; move use persists per creature/move until a new turn.
- Every affected target gets a defense window regardless of whether their hidden hand has a Dodge. A valid own Dodge consumes one card and cancels one settlement. Pass resolves that settlement. Surf creates separate target windows; one Dodge does not cancel both.
- Source-backed HP/injury accounting, Blaze, burn damage cap, active defense/power/speed reinforcement calculations and ordinary switching semantics. Level increases retain injuries/status/reinforcement and apply new max HP without healing previous injuries. Unavailable item/event sources cannot create new effects in this scenario; Wartortle/Blastoise's held-item suppression has no held item to affect here and full item interactions remain future work.
- Chlorophyll: optional once per creature/Play judgment and turn-local range permission. Leech Seed/Mega Drain heal after actual damage; Giga Drain also draws one. Fire-line moves judge after damage and apply burn only for intense sun and a valid status target. Rain Dish is optional in End and cures/heals on rain.
- Fainting preserves HP below zero, pauses normal play, and asks living trainers counterclockwise starting at the current turn's trainer. After all pass, defeat cleans attached state, awards the damage source's trainer a card when another Pokémon caused defeat, deploys an undefeated reserve without using ordinary switching, or eliminates the trainer and reveals identity. Source effects continue only after rescue/defeat resolution; immediate victory ends the chain.
- Rocket wins only as sole survivor when Leader is eliminated; otherwise Leader elimination gives Challengers victory. If no Challenger/Rocket remains, Leader/Gym team wins. Completed results disclose every identity, hand and reserve. Owner can return to lobby and rematch. Abandonment voting still clears game data without a false result/reveal.

Status hooks are implemented for turn-start flinch, sleep, confusion and poison, plus paralysis reinforcement inactivity. Those statuses/markers cannot be obtained from this command/dodge-only starter deck except burn. Conditional tests cover confusion's self-damage modifiers/replacement identity and paralysis retaining an inactive defense marker. Full status/item/event interaction coverage is not claimed.

## Decisions, timers and persistence

`pendingBattle` contains only public decision ID/kind/actor/target/clock. Private resolution data, deck order and undisclosed roles/hands/reserves remain server-owned. Server rejects unrelated actions until pending decisions settle; closing votes remain available. Normal action IDs/revisions protect costs, damage and responses from retries/stale submissions. A stale decision ID cannot answer a later window.

Response seconds now control real optional decision clocks in combat preview. They remain off by default. Turn clock pauses during other decisions; response clock pauses at the required actor's presence timeout or during closure voting. Expiry passes a response, never chooses a card. Restart preserves remaining time and pending identity. Polling/automatic passes do not refresh the seven-hour accepted-action lifetime. Browser response countdown is sampled on the existing polling interval; real-time remote latency/performance is not assessed here.
