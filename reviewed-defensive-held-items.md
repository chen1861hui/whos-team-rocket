# Leftovers, Poison Barb, and Bright Powder

The supplied older O06 item sheet shows the full effect paragraphs and weather icons. The saved Master Edition P05 and P07 posters corroborate the Bright Powder and Leftovers headings and show weather variants. Leftovers is clear on O06 and P07. Poison Barb is thunderstorm on O06. Bright Powder is heavy rain on O06 and thunderstorm on P05.

Leftovers heals the active holder by 1 HP at the end of its trainer’s turn. Gluttony replaces the effect with its own 1 HP heal. The shared end-turn guard limits the heal to one application.

Poison Barb applies Poison to the actual Pokémon that dealt positive damage. It uses the holder’s receiving-effect order and the existing status pipeline, including Shield Dust and Articuno. A source-free damage event supplies no Pokémon to poison. This uses the same surviving original holder timing as Rocky Helmet.

Bright Powder offers an optional judgment in the normal Dodge window. A final intense-sun or clear result counts as one virtual Dodge, so Keen Eye can convert it to damage reduction and X-Scissor can require another Dodge. The card remains equipped. The preview permits one attempt per settlement, including after an unsuccessful judgment. Actual Dodge options remain available after failure. Effects that prevent Dodge also prevent Bright Powder. Mewtwo’s Threat asks for an actual played response card under the existing event policy.

All three use the reviewed equip and replacement rules. The effect and weather data appear in the catalogue. Retail copy counts and the full weather distribution remain unverified. Isolated combat fixtures supply the item cards for testing.

Browser QA used three trainers and a spectator. A trainer equipped Leftovers through the item picker and healed at turn end. The defending trainer used Bright Powder to cancel a move after a clear judgment. In a separate round, a hit on the Poison Barb holder poisoned the original attacker. The spectator could see the result and had no defense controls.
