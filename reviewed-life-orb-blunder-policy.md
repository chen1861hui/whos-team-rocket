# Life Orb and Blunder Policy

The supplied older O06 sheet shows both paragraphs and thunderstorm icons. A previously reviewed Master Edition video also shows thunderstorm Life Orb around 23:00. O06 is `research-assets/user-older-edition-2026-11-04/share_33ea1aae55bf09c2a708ecde60b1e38b.png`, SHA-256 `38da1271a818c3dca1556f0046b9e3675ba44b0f54eac4ec6f34c0a227ebd845`.

Life Orb offers +1 when the holder deals damage. The target’s receiving modifiers then apply to the boosted damage. Accepting the boost schedules the 1 HP cost at the end of that damage settlement, even if a receiving effect reduces the final damage to zero. This is an interpretation of the printed timing. The HP loss can trigger rescue and follows the shared rules for ending the game. Burn can prevent the increase on a move. An event with a real Pokémon source, such as Rival’s Challenge, can also offer the boost.

Blunder Policy offers the source trainer a private choice of exactly two distinct hand cards after a Dodge fully cancels one settlement of its holder’s move. Paying resumes that same settlement at the damage or effect step. The Dodge remains spent. A virtual Dodge can qualify. Partial Dodge progress and Keen Eye’s damage reduction leave the settlement active and therefore do not trigger the item.

Both effects use the shared equip and replacement rules. The browser shows an optional boost and a private payment picker. The reviewed cards are available in isolated fixtures pending verification of retail quantities and weather distribution.
