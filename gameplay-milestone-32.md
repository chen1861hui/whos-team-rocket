## Engine and privacy contracts

Physical kind:event cards use eventType jigglypuff-performance or mr-mime-trick. self.battle.events adds targeting:'single' and safe public targetIds. Jiggly useEvent {cardId,targetId} has no effect/slot/array fields; Mr. Mime useEvent {cardId,targetId,effect:'hand'|'item'} validates the selected branch's own targets. Global Mew events retain cardId-only shape. All actions require own unblocked Play, actual own card, server authority and fresh revision; separate move/command/trait/switch allowances are untouched. A card spent as a weather cost never executes its event.

Jiggly may target self or any distant living active, not reserves, spectators or eliminated trainers. Effective active Early Bird prohibits both named event targets before payment; suppressed Early Bird permits them, and a reserve Kangaskhan does not protect its active partner. Judge for the target trainer, even when a pre-existing status, Shield Dust or Vital Spirit will prevent Sleep application. Drought replacements determine the final result; an empty supply creates no judgment and no Sleep. Apply Sleep via the shared one-status/immunity/Poison Touch path. No normal move defense/Dodge window, damage, Power/Defense consumption or offensive trait is introduced. Original target instance is pinned; a later replacement active does not inherit the status.

Mr. Mime requires another active within its user's card distance. Effective Speed, trainer cardRangePlayerId from Swift Swim/Chlorophyll and effective active Beedrill Sniper remove distance as their text grants. Paralysis disables Speed, Gas disables ungranted passive Sniper, and Dragonair's move-only reinforcement ignore does not disable Speed for a separate event card. Previously granted trainer range survives switching/Gas. This batch completes Chlorophyll's printed card-use grant: sunny judgment now stores cardRangePlayerId alongside the original instance move grant. Existing saved rangeCreatureId grants are recognized when the original granting Pokémon is active or reserve; turn reset still clears permissions.

Mr. Mime offers hand only for a real nonempty hand and item only for an actual held card. This meaningful-effect legality policy is documented separately from printed text. The actor chooses the branch and target before payment. Hand branch stores a private shuffled mapping and opens mandatory event-hand for the card user, exposing only anonymous {index} choices to that actor; everyone else sees public waiting, with no hidden card faces/IDs/shuffle/instance pins. respondBattle {decisionId,choice:'card',handIndex} rejects pass, extra fields, stale index/card/actor/target instance or vote. It transfers exactly that actual card through gainBattleCards and Pickup. Timeout pauses a mandatory hand choice without selecting.

Item branch removes the actual active-held card, then gains it into the event user's HAND through normal Pickup. It is neither discarded nor automatically equipped; ID/weather/metadata preserved. Losing the item offers effective active Mankey Defiant after transfer. Its optional draw2/no-hand-limit or pass completes before the resolving event card is discarded once. Optional expiry passes and finishes; vote/disconnect/restart rules remain. Event-created Defiant verifies exact response shape and original active instance; ordinary equip-triggered Defiant is unchanged. Existing finite-DECK_EMPTY policy for Defiant with fewer than2 cards stays covered by the prior Mankey tests.

Both events hold their one physical card outside piles until all continuations finish, preventing judgment/Pickup/Defiant from recycling it as its own supply. Shared status/gain/item-loss paths remain authoritative. No private resolving card or internal original-instance mapping is exported.
