Vital Spirit has two trait clauses: this Pokémon cannot acquire Sleep; during its Draw phase, it may instead privately inspect the top3 cards, obtain2 and put the remainder back on top. Both clauses are suppressed by Neutralizing Gas. Sleep immunity prevents new Sleep, rather than clearing an already attached status. Apply the immunity before the existing global status-replacement flow. This trait does not block other statuses.

The Draw clause says 改为, so it replaces the ordinary Draw operation; it is not an extra3-card peek followed by two ordinary draws. Explicit preview policies: offer once for the entire own Draw phase, before any ordinary draw; accepting consumes that phase's ordinary draw obligation and creates a mandatory private choice. The printed fixed gain2 remains2 when Flinch reduced the ordinary draw count. This Flinch interaction is an interpretation: the face gives no separate rule for changed draw counts. Passing or beginning an ordinary draw gives up this phase's replacement. No use during Preparation, Play, another trainer's Draw, or while the original actor is suppressed/inactive.

Legacy-save policy: replacement eligibility requires an explicit `drawStarted:false`, which a fresh turn initializes. A saved turn from before this field existed conservatively retains normal Draw only for that current phase; its missing history cannot prove whether an ordinary draw already occurred. In particular, a remaining count of1 could mean a partial normal Draw or a fresh Flinch-reduced Draw. The next own turn resets eligibility normally. This prevents a legacy partial Draw from combining an already drawn card with the full replacement gain.

Inspect actual available cards, with the established finite-supply refill policy; when fewer than3 exist, inspect the available number and obtain min(2, available). Keep the unchosen real card on top. This finite-supply handling is interpretation, not an extra printed sentence. Private inspection is neither public reveal nor judgment, so Drought does not run. After accepting, selection cannot pass or timeout-pick. Store private card identities and original Pokémon/trainer binding through votes, restart and exact retries; expose indexed safe card faces only to the actor. Gain the chosen cards as one batch. This gain occurs in the actor's own Draw phase and does not trigger Pickup. Do not fabricate cards or leak the unchosen card to other trainers.

Intimidate says 你弃置, without 可以: it is mandatory. The entering Tauros's trainer chooses any living trainer with a hand, including themselves, with no printed distance restriction, then discards one actual card from that trainer. The actor may see their own hand faces; another trainer's hand uses hidden randomized slots. This is not a command or move and cannot be canceled by Dodge. Empty hands are excluded as targets; if all living hands are empty, the event completes without a discard. Once a legal target/selection exists, there is no optional pass or timeout selection. Target eligibility and actual card identity are revalidated before mutation.

Booklet07 p08 defines entry as either being the active Pokémon when the game begins, or moving from reserve to active. Booklet04 p03 deals the starting four cards before the game formally begins. Therefore the initial Tauros trigger resolves against starting hands before the first trainer's turn, as well as after every successful ordinary switch, move-effect switch or forced deployment. Level-up, reserve revelation alone, failed switching and canceled switching are not new entries. Reserve traits remain inactive. Trainer-wide Gas suppression follows a reserve into the active zone and prevents its entry trait while suppressed; an already paid suppression does not end when its source leaves active play.

Initial entry events use the leader-first counterclockwise seat order as an explicit simultaneous-start ordering policy, following the booklet's normal current-trainer-first direction. Retain a saved entry queue and bound actor instance across initial setup, Arena Trap payments, successful swaps, defeat/deployment, pending parent damage and turn-start continuations. Finish entry effects before resuming the interrupted operation. A newly deployed Tauros must not make the parent move's remaining hits target that replacement. Repeated actual entries can trigger again; there is no printed once-per-turn limit.

Thunderbolt and Electroweb trigger only after positive damage, using a real judgment whose final weather must be 雷雨 / thunderstorm. Clear or intense sun cannot satisfy this check. Body Slam instead accepts both sunny weather types: 烈日 / intense sun and 晴朗 / clear. Each Electroweb target settles separately and has its own after-damage judgment. Zero/canceled damage produces no judgment; damage rescue/defeat precedes source-after-damage effects. Preserve original targets and the existing Drought/status-immunity/Poison Touch continuations. The Electabuzz2 paragraph omits 而 before 造成; the other quoted paragraphs retain their printed wording.

Earthquake removes one reinforcement marker from the original surviving damaged target after positive damage, before that target's receiving traits. The source trainer chooses the marker. No marker means no selection; zero/canceled damage means no removal. Removing one Power removes one physical marker even when Scyther has stacked copies. Reinforcement removal is move text, so Gas does not disable it. The two-target restriction is at most two distinct active Pokémon within the move user's normal distance, including self when otherwise legal. All moves retain the booklet's once-per-Pokémon/move/Play and one-command-using-Pokémon/Play rules, with a real command card per use. `paralysis-judgment` on these command moves means damage followed by a judgment, not the existing non-damaging weather-paid Thunder Wave settlement.
